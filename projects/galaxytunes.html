Project Overview

GalaxyTunes is a modern music and video player application designed to provide users with a smooth and seamless multimedia experience.

The application includes dedicated sections for discovering content, searching for music and videos, managing a personal library and accessing user profile information.

The playback experience was designed to support continuous listening with a modern mini-player and media controls.

My responsibilities included Flutter development, audio and video playback, API integration, Firebase integration, state management and overall application functionality.
