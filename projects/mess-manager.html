01

Project Overview

Mess Manager is a mobile application designed to simplify the daily management of shared living arrangements.

The application allows mess managers to manage members, meals, bazar entries and expenses from a centralized dashboard.

Members can track their meals, expenses and monthly calculations without relying on manual notebooks or spreadsheets.

Role Flutter Developer
Platform Android / iOS
Type Management App
Year 2026
02

User Roles

Manager

Managers can create and manage messes, add members, manage meals, add bazar entries, track expenses and monitor monthly reports.

Member

Members can view their meal records, expenses, bazar information and monthly mess calculations.

03

Key Features

Member Management

Add, remove and manage mess members from a centralized system.

Meal Management

Track breakfast, lunch and dinner for every member.

Bazar Management

Record daily grocery purchases, categories, buyers and amounts.

Monthly Calculation

Calculate meal rates and individual monthly expenses automatically.

Reports

View useful summaries of meals, expenses and bazar activities.

Role Based Access

Different permissions are provided for managers and members.

Challenges & Solutions

Meal Calculation

One of the important challenges was creating a reliable calculation system for different meal counts and monthly expenses.

Multiple User Roles

Manager and member permissions were separated to make sure users could only access the features relevant to their roles.

Real-Time Data

Firebase services were used to keep important mess information synchronized between users.
