Project Overview

Roots & Routes is a feature-rich mobile travel application designed to provide users with a smarter, more connected and personalized travel experience.

The app uses Google Maps to help users discover nearby attractions and places, while providing real-time weather updates, currency conversion, and travel-related information.

Users can also interact with the AI Concierge through both text and voice to get instant travel assistance and recommendations.

The application also includes a community and real-time chat system, image-to-text translation, push notifications, subscription-based features, and other tools designed to make travel planning and exploration easier from a single platform.

I worked on the Flutter mobile application, implementing the user interface, application logic, AI text and voice features, Google Maps integration, real-time chat, notifications, translation, currency conversion, subscription system, and App Store deployment.

Challenges & Solutions

AI Usage & Cost Management

One of the major challenges was controlling AI usage while maintaining a good user experience. Usage limits and subscription-based access were implemented to keep AI costs predictable.

Subscription Management

RevenueCat was integrated to manage subscription products, entitlements and user access across the application.

App Store Deployment

The application was prepared, tested through TestFlight and successfully published to the Apple App Store.
