Project Overview

AI Document Helper is designed to help students and professionals understand large documents faster using artificial intelligence.

Users can upload PDFs or document images and receive AI-generated summaries, important points, questions and learning materials.

Instead of simply reading a document, users can interact with their content and turn it into an active learning experience.

Challenges & Solutions

Processing large documents efficiently requires careful text extraction and chunking before sending content to AI models.

Learning Experience

The application was designed around a complete learning workflow instead of providing only document summarization.
