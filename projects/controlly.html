Project Overview

Controlly is a privacy-focused digital wellbeing application designed to give users greater control over their social media experience.

The application allows users to hide and block distracting or unwanted features across popular social media platforms including YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and Twitter/X.

Instead of completely blocking social media, Controlly gives users more granular control over which features they want to disable. This allows users to keep the useful parts of their favorite platforms while removing unnecessary distractions.

Users can control features such as short-form videos, reels, explore and suggested content, comments, stories and direct messaging depending on the supported platform.

The application also provides activity tracking and customizable controls to help users build a cleaner, more focused and personalized digital environment.

I worked on the Flutter mobile application, implementing the user interface, application logic, social platform controls, activity tracking, Firebase integration, notifications, subscription functionality and cross-platform mobile experience.

Challenges & Solutions

Platform Feature Control

One of the main challenges was providing users with granular control over different social media features. The application was designed to allow individual features to be enabled or disabled instead of forcing users to completely block an entire platform.

Cross-Platform Experience

Supporting different social platforms and their individual feature controls required a flexible architecture and carefully structured application logic to provide a consistent user experience.

Privacy & Digital Wellbeing

The application was designed around user privacy, personal control and digital wellbeing, ensuring that users could customize their social media experience according to their own needs.
